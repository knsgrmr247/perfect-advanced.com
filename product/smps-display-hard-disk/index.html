Product Application & Standards

SMPS (Switched Mode Power Supply): Provides regulated power to CCTV cameras and recorders. Designed for efficiency and stability, it protects equipment from voltage fluctuations and surges, ensuring long-term reliability. Display Monitors: These are used for live viewing and playback of recorded footage. They support HDMI/VGA input and come in various sizes (typically from 18.5” to 32”), offering clear visual output for surveillance environments. Hard Disks: Installed in DVRs/NVRs, these are built for 24/7 operation and continuous video recording. Surveillance-grade hard drives ensure durability, high write-speed, and extended storage capacity, usually ranging from 500GB to 10TB.
