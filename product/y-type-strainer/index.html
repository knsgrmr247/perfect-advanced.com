Product Application & Standards

Y-Type Strainers are essential components for protecting downstream equipment such as valves, pumps, and meters from debris and particulate matter in the pipeline. Designed for durability and easy maintenance, these strainers are suitable for steam, gas, and liquid applications across industrial and utility sectors. Available in cast iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, and alloy constructions, they are built to handle pressure ratings up to 300#
